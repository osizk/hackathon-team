# Price test part 2: summary, paying with crypto

The 35 comparable items from part 1, paid with crypto instead of a normal
card. Ways to pay: [crypto-options.md](crypto-options.md). Every row:
[crypto-results.md](crypto-results.md). Part 1: [summary.md](summary.md).
Read 2026-10-15. 1 USD = 33.62 THB, as in part 1.

## Read this first

- **No page could be opened.** Every fee and price is from a search result,
  tagged [search]. Many fee numbers come from blogs, not the card issuer.
- We use two cases. **Cautious**: only stated fees, no cashback. **Best
  case**: one card's 2% cashback and fee refund both hold. Each rests on one
  source.
- The U-Card's fees, FX and cashback were not found. So no number here is
  about the U-Card.

## Headline

| Case | Cheaper with crypto | Same (within 0.5%) | Dearer with crypto | Typical difference | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cautious | 0 | 0 | 35 | +23 THB (+2.4%) median | +3 to +463 THB (+0.9% to +2.5%) |
| Best case | 35 | 0 | 0 | -26 THB (-1.8%) median | -2 to -222 THB (-0.8% to -1.8%) |

- **Cautious**: crypto costs more for every item. At a Thai shop it is about
  +2.4% (RedotPay: 2.2% FX and conversion on a baht charge, plus 0.25% to
  buy USDT). At a shop abroad that charges US dollars it is less, about
  +1.1%, because there is no FX fee.
- **Best case**: crypto saves 0.8% to 1.8% on every item. This needs the
  Bitget Wallet Card's 2% cashback and its fee-free allowance of USD 400 a
  month (13,448 THB). Above the allowance, fees come back: the Sigma lens
  (30) and Garmin watch (42) save less.
- **In baht the difference is small**: a median of 23 THB more (cautious) or
  26 THB less (best case). Across all 35 items it adds up to 2,537 THB more
  or 1,766 THB less.
- Compare this with part 1: the agent's compare between Thai shops saved a
  median 225 THB (20%). That is about ten times the crypto effect either way.

## Which route wins

| Route | Wins, cautious | Wins, best case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RedotPay card | 35 | 0 |
| Bitget Wallet Card | 0 | 35 |
| Gift card bought with crypto (Lazada, Shopee, Central) | 0 | 0 |
| Direct crypto at a shop (Newegg, BitPay) | 0 | 0 |
| U-Card | not computable | not computable |

- **Gift cards never won.** At best they add 0.25% and give 1% cashback
  that is not confirmed. They also exist only for a few shops: Lazada,
  Shopee, Central. Shopee cards go up to 250 THB only.
- **Direct crypto never won.** No Thai shop on the list takes crypto. Thai
  SEC rules stop licensed firms from running crypto payments to shops. The
  only shop abroad found was Newegg, and its prices were higher (items 27,
  28, 34, 49).
- **No crypto discount** was found at any shop on the list.

## Does crypto flip any item from Thailand to abroad?

- **Not for real.** One item flips on paper: the Logitech MX Master 3S (27).
  With the cautious fees, the Thai shop pays 2.45% and Amazon.com pays
  1.25%. So Amazon.com comes out 31 THB cheaper (2,766 versus 2,797).
- But the Amazon.com price is a floor, with shipping counted as 0. Any
  shipping above about 28 THB undoes the flip. The Amazon fee part 1 found
  (Amazon.de to Asia, about 710 THB) is far higher. So the Thai shop almost
  surely still wins.
- In the best case, nothing flips. The same cashback applies on both sides.
- The 10 items that were cheaper abroad in part 1 stay cheaper abroad. They
  are still open until a shipping fee is read (part 1 caveat).

## What the presenter can say on stage

Three plain lines:

1. "Paying with crypto does not make things cheaper by itself. With the
   fees we could confirm, a crypto card costs about 2% more than a Thai bank
   card."
2. "The saving comes from the agent comparing shops on the real total. In
   our test that was 225 baht, or 20%, per item in the middle case."
3. "CryptoMate lets you spend the crypto you already hold, safely: rules in
   code, and nothing pays without your approve."

What not to claim:

- Not "cheaper with crypto" or "crypto discount". We found no shop discount
  for crypto, and the cautious case is dearer for all 35 items.
- Not "no fees" or "no FX fees" for the U-Card. That is U-topia's marketing
  line. We could not find its fee terms.
- Not a cashback percent for the U-Card. U-topia publishes none.
- Not "we pay the shop in crypto". The shop gets baht from the card. Paying
  Thai shops in crypto directly runs against Thai SEC rules.
- Not "buying abroad with crypto saves money". It flips no item for real.

## What could not be checked

- **No page was opened.** Every fact is [search]. Fees change often.
- **U-Card**: fees, FX, cashback, card currency and deposit networks were
  not found. Read them in the U-topia app before the demo. If the U-Card has
  0% FX on baht, the cautious cost drops to about the 0.25% on-ramp.
- **Bitget Wallet Card**: the 2-3% cashback, its token, and the fee-free
  allowance come from one source each.
- **RedotPay for Thai residents**: Thai users report it works. No official
  country list was found.
- **Network match**: the cheap route is USDT on BNB Smart Chain from Bitkub
  (0.01 USDT, from a fee-listing site). RedotPay's own help page only
  confirms Tron, and Bitkub's Tron fee was not found.
- **USDT price in baht** versus the dollar rate: within about 0.3%, counted
  as 0.
- **Card FX fee in part 1**: part 1 counted 0% card fee at shops abroad. A
  Thai bank card usually charges an FX fee there. That was not checked. If
  it is higher than 1.25%, the crypto card is cheaper than the bank card for
  the 10 items bought abroad.
- **Gift card fees**: Bitrefill says most cards sell at face value. A review
  says 0-3%. We used 3% in the cautious case.
- **Amazon US gift card** bought with crypto: whether it pays for an order
  shipped to Thailand is not confirmed.
- **Newegg** shipping to Thailand was not confirmed.
- **Part 1 prices** were reused. The subagents saw two possible changes:
  item 8 may be 441-490 THB at JIB (below part 1's 569), and item 15 may be
  599 THB and out of stock at HomePro (above part 1's 489). Item 36 is
  listed at HLJ Japan for 26.36 USD but out of stock. We did not change part
  1's numbers.
