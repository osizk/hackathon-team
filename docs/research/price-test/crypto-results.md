# Price test part 2: results, paying with crypto

The 35 items that part 1 could compare, now paid with crypto. Part 1 rows are
in [results.md](results.md). The ways to pay and their fees are in
[crypto-options.md](crypto-options.md). The summary is in
[crypto-summary.md](crypto-summary.md).

## How to read this file

- **Best card total, part 1**: the lower of part 1's Thai total and world
  floor, paid by a normal card with no fee. "(TH)" is a Thai shop, "(W
  floor)" is a shop abroad with international shipping counted as 0.
- **Best crypto total**: the same items paid with crypto, baht-to-USDT cost
  included. We check both the Thai shop and the shop abroad from part 1, and
  take the lower. We also checked gift-card and direct-crypto routes for each
  item.
- **Cautious**: RedotPay card, +2.2% on a baht (or euro, pound, Singapore
  dollar) charge, +1.0% on a US dollar charge, plus +0.25% for baht to USDT
  on Bitkub. No cashback.
- **Best case**: Bitget Wallet Card with 2% cashback and fees refunded
  inside a USD 400 a month allowance, plus 0.25% baht to USDT. Each claim
  rests on one source. Treat it as the most crypto could save.
- The card fee is only on the amount paid to the shop. Import duty and 7% VAT
  are paid at customs, so they get no card fee.
- **Difference**: crypto total minus card total. A plus sign means the card
  is cheaper. A minus sign means crypto is cheaper.
- **Winner**: "Card" or "Crypto", first for the cautious case, then the best
  case. "Same" means within 0.5%. "flips" means crypto makes the other side
  (Thailand or abroad) the cheapest.
- **FX rate**: part 1's 1 USD = 33.62 THB, and part 1's ECB rates for other
  currencies.
- **U-Card**: its fees were not found, so no row uses it. See
  [crypto-options.md](crypto-options.md#1-crypto-cards-for-thai-residents).
- **Tags**: [seen] means the page was opened. [search] means only a search
  result. No page could be opened in this session, so every tag is [search].
- **Date read**: 2026-10-15 for every row.
- Rows were filled in four batches by subagents: 10, 10, 10 and 5 items.
