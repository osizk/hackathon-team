the route CryptoMate uses.

does not.

**Thai law.** Since 1 April 2022, Thai SEC rules stop licensed crypto firms
from building tools for paying with crypto or opening shop wallets. Buying
and trading crypto stays legal. No repeal found
([Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/5e623d48)
[search]). TouristDigiPay, a test scheme announced 18 August 2025, is for
foreign tourists only. It turns crypto into baht e-money, so the shop still
gets baht
([Vixio](https://www.vixio.com/insights/pc-thailand-launches-sandbox-tourist-crypto-baht-payments)
[search]). So a Thai resident cannot pay a Thai shop in crypto directly.
Paying by crypto card, as CryptoMate does, is the realistic route.

No crypto discount was found at any shop on our list, in Thailand or abroad.

## Rates used in crypto-results.md

The same rates are used for every item. Two cases:

- **Cautious**: only fees that a source states, no cashback that only one
  source claims, no fee allowance.
- **Best case**: Bitget's 2% cashback and fee-free allowance both hold. This
  rests on one source each. Treat it as an upper bound on the benefit.

| Route | Cautious, THB or non-USD charge | Cautious, USD charge | Best case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baht to USDT (Bitkub, BSC) | +0.25% | +0.25% | +0.25% |
| RedotPay card | +2.2% | +1.0% | +2.2% / +1.0% |
| Bitget Wallet Card | +3.23% (1.7 + 0.53 + 1 top-up) | +1.53% | -2.0% inside a USD 400 (13,448 THB) monthly allowance; fees above it |
| Gift card (Bitrefill, Lazada or Central) | +3% (top of the 0-3% fee range) | - | -1% (unconfirmed cashback) |
| Direct crypto (Newegg, BitPay) | - | +0% plus network fee | +0% |
| U-Card | Not computable | Not computable | Not computable |

- The card fee is on the amount paid to the shop only. Thai import duty and
  VAT are not paid with the card, so they do not get the fee.
- So the cautious best route is always RedotPay: +2.45% on a baht charge and
  +1.25% on a dollar charge, on-ramp included. The best case is always
  Bitget: -1.75% inside the allowance.
